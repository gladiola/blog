title:  "Picking a Good Boss"
date:   2018-06-15 08:30:00
description: Recognizing the Other Half of the Interview Cycle
---


One of the more important points to remember, however, will be that everyone in the employment process will have their own perspective.  Since the structure of employment, and mechanisms for finding and securing a job, all have inherent imbalances in power, what may seem negative to one may be positive for another.  Also, some people do not recognize that they are each an equally powerful party in the decisions.  When interviews are taking place, all of the people involved are evaluating each other.  Some managers may seem resistent to the idea that they are being interviewed by the applicant; but, if you want to prevent losses associated with working for the wrong boss, then it's up to you to find out if you want to spend your time on another's decisions.  Are these people about to hire you qualified to be your boss?  Will they be a good fit?  Would you want to waste 30 percent of your life on a very bad fit?  It's up to you pick a good boss.
